# ADR-0003: Research Vault Stores Derived Knowledge Only

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-08-23

## Context

Research projects need structured notes, evidence records, comparisons, and synthesis that do not belong in bibliographic metadata.

## Decision

Use the Research Vault for derived scientific knowledge and project reasoning. Do not duplicate Zotero-managed PDFs by default or treat summaries as primary evidence.

## Consequences

Every important vault record should link back to authoritative source identifiers and locations.
