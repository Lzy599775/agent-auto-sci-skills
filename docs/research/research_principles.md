# Research Principles

## Priorities

Evidence integrity and reproducibility take precedence over speed or narrative polish.

## Operating rules

1. Start from a defined research question, decision, or verification target.
2. Route the task before selecting tools.
3. Prefer authoritative primary material and verify unstable information.
4. Preserve source material and distinguish it from derived artifacts.
5. Record assumptions, exclusions, transformations, and uncertainty.
6. Treat missing information honestly; never complete a schema by guessing.
7. Verify numerical values and citation support before synthesis.
8. Separate Results, Discussion, and Conclusion boundaries.
9. Use quality gates that protect validity, integrity, reproducibility, security, or traceability.
10. Record durable decisions without creating ceremonial bureaucracy.

## Autonomy boundary

Codex may search, inspect, extract, analyze, test, debug, and create derived files in an approved workspace. External writes, destructive actions, publication, restricted-data upload, Zotero library mutation, and scientific decisions with materially different defensible options require explicit authority.
