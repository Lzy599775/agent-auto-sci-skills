# Academic Claim Standard

## Core rule

`CLAIM STRENGTH <= EVIDENCE STRENGTH`

## Prohibited escalations

- Association must not become causation.
- A single-city or selected-sample finding must not become a universal mechanism.
- Model interpretation or feature importance must not become an empirical causal mechanism.
- Statistical significance must not become practical significance.
- Absence of evidence must not become evidence of absence.
- Prediction accuracy must not become explanation or policy effectiveness.
- A non-significant estimate must not be described as proof of no effect.

## Required qualifiers

Preserve population, geography, period, design, measurement, comparator, uncertainty, and analytical assumptions when they materially bound a claim.

## Revision rule

When support is weaker than the claim, narrow the claim, add qualifiers, obtain stronger evidence, or remove it. Do not strengthen prose to sound decisive.
