# Research Idea Evaluation

## Procedure

1. Convert the broad topic into a population/context, exposure/intervention, outcome, mechanism, scale, and time frame where applicable.
2. Search current literature and active datasets/projects to determine what is established, contested, and missing.
3. Separate novelty of topic, data, method, theory, scale, and decision relevance.
4. Evaluate feasibility: data access, measurement validity, sample support, ethics, time, computation, and required expertise.
5. Identify alternative explanations, bias pathways, and falsifiable predictions.
6. Compare at least two defensible designs when they lead to materially different conclusions.
7. Recommend proceed, refine, pilot, defer, or reject with explicit evidence and uncertainty.

## Outputs

- Research-question record.
- Novelty and evidence-gap map.
- Feasibility and risk assessment.
- Candidate designs and quality gates.
- Bounded recommendation.
