# Citation-Claim Audit

## Objective

Determine whether a cited source supports the exact claim made at the cited location.

## Procedure

1. Capture the claim verbatim and identify its type: descriptive, numeric, methodological, associative, causal, mechanistic, or generalizing.
2. Resolve the citation to the authoritative source and correct version.
3. Read the relevant full-text location, including qualifiers and population/context limits.
4. Create an evidence record with source location and faithful excerpt or summary.
5. Classify support as `DIRECT_SUPPORT`, `PARTIAL_SUPPORT`, `CONTEXTUAL_SUPPORT`, `CONTRADICTORY`, or `NOT_SUPPORTED`.
6. Check whether claim strength exceeds evidence strength.
7. Recommend keep, narrow, qualify, replace citation, add citation, or remove claim.

## Numeric checks

Verify value, unit, denominator, uncertainty interval, subgroup, time period, and whether the number is observed, modeled, or inferred.

## Output

Return the classification, confidence, source location, rationale, and a bounded revision when needed.
