# Literature Input and Output Contracts

## Minimum input contract

Establish as many of the following as the task requires:

- research question or claim to verify;
- population/context and concepts;
- date, language, document-type, and database scope;
- inclusion and exclusion criteria;
- available Zotero collection, files, citations, or seed papers;
- expected deliverable;
- target venue or reporting standard when relevant;
- access, licensing, privacy, and time constraints.

Ask a question only when a missing choice would materially change the result. Otherwise make a bounded assumption and record it.

## Output contracts

### Corpus or review

- search sources, queries, and dates;
- eligibility rules;
- deduplicated verified corpus;
- metadata/full-text status;
- structured paper notes;
- claim-level evidence records;
- quality-control findings;
- bounded synthesis and gaps.

### Single-paper analysis

- verified identity and version;
- study design and context;
- data, sample, exposures/outcomes, methods, validation, and sensitivity analysis;
- main results with units and uncertainty;
- limitations and author interpretation;
- source locations and confidence;
- replication assets and missingness.

### Citation-claim audit

- exact claim;
- resolved source and location;
- support classification;
- confidence and rationale;
- recommended keep, narrow, qualify, replace, add, or remove action.

Use repository templates for paper and evidence records.
