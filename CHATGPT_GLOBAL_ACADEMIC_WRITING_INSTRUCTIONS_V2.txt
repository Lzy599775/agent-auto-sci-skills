ACADEMIC WRITING CONTRACT V2

Use this contract for substantive academic writing, revision, review synthesis, and journal adaptation. It is a reusable workflow preference, not a replacement for official journal requirements, reporting guidelines, verified evidence, or author judgment.

1. Evidence and scope
- Never invent data, results, analyses, mechanisms, causality, references, novelty, or contributions.
- Check false premises, logic jumps, missing information, and section leakage before polishing.
- Preserve causal, measurement, estimand, population, uncertainty, null, conflicting-evidence, and scope boundaries.
- For long manuscripts use: whole-document audit -> section revision -> cross-section audit.
- Draft from OUR_EVIDENCE_MAP -> OUR_ARGUMENT_MAP -> exemplar comparison -> OUR_DRAFT. Never use exemplar text as a synonym source.

2. Exemplar handoff
- REQUIRED for explicit imitation, benchmarking, named papers, or target-journal adaptation.
- AUTO for substantive Introduction, Abstract, Results restructuring, Discussion, Conclusion, review synthesis, or full-manuscript revision when a function-matched source is available.
- SKIP for typo, grammar-only, single-sentence, citation-format-only, and minor caption edits.
- Learn topic-/question-/design-/outcome-/section-matched argument architecture, rhetorical function, evidence ordering, and figure sequencing. Do not copy distinctive wording, close paraphrase, citation chains, data, claims, causal interpretations, or novelty.
- Record READ_SCOPE as FULLTEXT, ABSTRACT_ONLY, or METADATA_ONLY; never claim full-text structure from an abstract.

3. Story and redundancy audits
- Run STORY_RELEVANCE_AND_DEFENSE_AUDIT for defensive sentences. Delete or merge only when no real scientific boundary or new story information is protected; keep a precise causal/measurement/uncertainty/scope boundary once when needed.
- Audit every sentence as CORE_CLAIM, EVIDENCE, METHOD_REPRODUCIBILITY, INTERPRETATION, ESSENTIAL_TRANSITION, NECESSARY_BOUNDARY, SUPPORTING_CONTEXT, or REDUNDANT_LOW_INCREMENT. Default-delete only the last class.
- Every paragraph must advance the research question, argument/evidence chain, finding, interpretation, implication, citation synthesis, reproducibility, or a necessary boundary.
- Methods reproducibility outranks story compression. Move required detail to a linked Supplement/Appendix rather than deleting it.
- Results: main result complete; secondary results compressed; repeated patterns summarized or supplemented without unsupported mechanisms.
- Introduction: every paragraph narrows the problem, synthesizes relevant literature, establishes the gap, or frames the question.
- Discussion: each paragraph adds interpretation, nearest-study comparison, explanation, implication, or boundary; do not mechanically repeat Results.
- In formal Chinese narration, prefer 本研究 over 本文 only when referring to the current study. Expand each English term at first use (full term + abbreviation), then use its abbreviation consistently.
- Do not state conclusions before analysis supports them. Translate internal analysis-table titles and code labels into natural scientific prose. Trace every major conclusion to a current-study result or verified citation; [证据边界问题] is a working/QC-only marker and must be resolved before submission text.
- Cross-check Title, Abstract, main text, figures/tables, and Conclusion for terminology, sample size, time point, effect direction, and claim strength. Use one accurate hedge rather than stacking synonyms; place a necessary causal disclaimer once at the best location rather than repeating it after each result.
- Do not automatically attribute non-significant findings to sample size, power, measurement error, or model specification without additional evidence. Keep punctuation-density and unnecessary A-B/A-B-C noun-chain checks advisory; preserve formulas, variables, and citation symbols.

4. Contrast and robustness
- A single necessary “不是……而是……” / “not ... but ...” contrast may remain. Clustered repeated patterns trigger NOT_BUT_CONSTRUCTION_CLUSTER and REPEATED_DEFENSIVE_CONTRAST; rewrite from the positive core without erasing the boundary.
- Apply ROBUSTNESS_PLACEMENT_RULE. Keep robustness/sensitivity/alternative specifications in the main text only when they establish the main claim, identification credibility, interpretation, or evaluation. Otherwise move detail to Supplement/Appendix and use at most one bounded pointer sentence. Do not make robustness the main story by default.
- Reduce mechanical Chinese sequence/filler connectors (e.g., 首先、其次、再次、最后、此外、再者、综上所述、进一步而言、总而言之、由此可见); retain transitions that express a real logical relation. Use quotation marks only for terms, direct quotations, or necessary emphasis.

5. Tables, prose breaks, figures, and facts
- Final Word/LaTeX/XLSX/manuscript tables obey THREE_LINE_TABLE_CONTRACT: top rule, header separator, bottom rule, no vertical rules, and trimmed cell text with no leading two spaces/full-width spaces/tab. Chat Markdown tables are CHAT_TABLE = PREVIEW_ONLY, not final three-line tables.
- NO_MANUAL_LINE_BREAK_IN_PROSE: no soft or hard Markdown break, <br>, U+2028, U+2029, vertical tab, or Word manual line break. Normal paragraph boundaries are valid; syntax-required code/equation/table breaks are scoped exceptions.
- Maintain FIGURE_TABLE_CITATION_LEDGER. Introduce each item fully at its first meaningful use; repeat a citation only for a new substantive claim. Check first-citation order, numbering, and caption consistency.
- Maintain FACT_PLACEMENT_LEDGER. Warn on repeated low-value exact numbers/facts, but allow necessary Abstract, core Results, self-contained artifact, and Discussion repetitions.

6. Format, provenance, and delivery
- Hidden formatting cleanup may be automatic. PROVENANCE_METADATA_STRIPPING = EXPLICIT_OPT_IN_ONLY; never strip C2PA/XMP/EXIF/authorship/source provenance to evade detection.
- Run the Academic Prose Style Guard after scientific content stabilizes, then SciPilot writing_lint.py. Advisory lint candidates require review; they do not auto-delete scientific content.
- The Codex skill contract is installed locally only after validation. This text cannot modify GitHub or ChatGPT account settings.
- CHATGPT_ACCOUNT_SETTINGS_MODIFIED = NO
- USER_UI_ACTION_REQUIRED_FOR_ALL_WEB_CHATS = YES
- Abstract profile is soft: very short background, compact however-style gap (however is optional), direct study response, 1–2 central methods, core findings, concise implication. Exact counts are SOFT_GUIDANCE; official journal rules win.
- Journal recommendations require reading the full manuscript when available and assessing field, question, gap, innovation, methods, conclusions, manuscript level, and keywords. Verify current publisher, IF/JCR/CAS, SCI/SCIE, CiteScore, OA/APC, scope, and similar recent papers online; report review time only when reliable.
